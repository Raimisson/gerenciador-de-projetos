## Carta de resposta

Dear Editor,

We thank the editor and the reviewers for their careful reading of the manuscript. Below we respond point by point; changes are indicated with their location in the revised manuscript.

**R1.1 (Reviewer 1).** _The conclusions are too cautious. The authors should state clearly that decoupling increases energy efficiency spending, since this is well established._

**Response.** We thank the reviewer for this comment. We agree that the question is central for regulators. However, the only study in our evidence base that reports on this relationship describes it, in its abstract, as an association, and its empirical design could not be assessed without the full text. Stating a causal effect would therefore go beyond the evidence we were able to verify. We have made this reasoning explicit in the Discussion.
_Justification._ A evidência disponível (E-0001) é de nível abstract e usa linguagem de associação; afirmar efeito causal exagera a conclusão. Regras editoriais e pedidos de revisores não justificam exagero de conclusão.
_Change:_ Nova frase na Discussão sobre o desenho do estudo de Kahn-Lang (2016) não ter podido ser avaliado. — _Location:_ Seção 4, parágrafo P-D1

**R1.2 (Reviewer 1).** _Please read and discuss von Loessl and Wetzel (2022) and Datta (2019), which appear to be directly relevant._

**Response.** We agree and thank the reviewer. We have obtained and reviewed both studies and now report their designs and results in Sections 3 and 4. [PENDING: complete after full-text extraction; do not anticipate their findings]
_Change:_ [PENDENTE: depende da leitura dos textos integrais — não antecipar resultados] — _Location:_ Seções 3 e 4 (a definir)

**R2.1 (Reviewer 2).** _The search relies on a single index. Please justify this choice or extend the search._

**Response.** We thank the reviewer. We have extended the search to additional sources that were accessible to us and documented all searches, including unsuccessful attempts, in the search log. Sources that could not be accessed are now listed explicitly as a limitation.
_Justification._ A extensão depende do acesso às bases; o que não for acessível será declarado como limitação, sem alegar cobertura que não houve.
_Change:_ Methods e Limitations atualizados; search log com novas buscas. — _Location:_ Seções 2 e 5

**Response.** We agree. The abstract now states explicitly that no study could be formally included and why.
